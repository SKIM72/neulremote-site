Get started

Delete your NeulRemote account

How to delete your NeulRemote account and your data on the NeulRemote server. Deletion can't be undone.

In the app

  • Open the NeulRemote app → Profile (top right) → Delete account → enter your password → [Delete account].

On the web

  • Sign in to the web app → Delete account next to your account (on a phone screen: Account → Account management → Delete account) → enter your password → [Delete account].

If you can't use the app

  • Email support@neulremote.com from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm it's you, delete it and reply.

What is deleted

  • Right away: your account (email, name, password hash, role), sign-in sessions, registered phones and their status, remote help links, connection history, profile photo, push addresses and trusted sign-in devices. Registered phones stop waiting for remote access and are unregistered.

What remains

  • Error reports lose their link to your account (emails, tokens and IP addresses are already masked) and are deleted after 30 days.
  • A record that an account was deleted (an internal ID and the time, no email) is kept for security.
  • Server backups for recovery may keep deleted information for up to 30 days. Backups older than 30 days are deleted automatically.
  • Pairing keys, access history and settings stored only on your phone are removed when you uninstall the app.
