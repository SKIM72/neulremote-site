NeulRemote security

NeulRemote lets one device see and control another, so security comes first. Here is where your data goes, and what the server can and can't see.

Screen, sound and input are encrypted between devices

  • The screen, sound, touch and keyboard input, files and voice calls travel encrypted between the two devices with WebRTC (DTLS and SRTP). Only those two devices hold the keys.
  • Over the internet, if the devices can't reach each other directly, the connection goes through the NeulRemote relay server (TURN). It only forwards the encrypted bytes, so the server can't read or store the session.

What the server knows — and what it doesn't

  • It knows: your account (email, name, a one-way hash of your password), your registered devices and their status (model, Android version, battery, last seen) and what's needed to link two devices (which device asked to connect, and when).
  • It doesn't know: what's on the screen, the sound, what you type, the files you send or what's said on a call. It never sees your password itself. Details are in the privacy policy.

Using NeulRemote without the server

  • On the same Wi‑Fi, or over USB, devices connect directly — no account, no server. The first time on Wi‑Fi you pair by checking that both screens show the same six digits.

"Don't use the relay server"

  • Turn on Don't use the relay server (app: Settings → Remote screen; web app: 설정 → 원격 화면 → 중계 서버 거치지 않기) and internet connections also go only directly between devices, never through the relay.
  • Networks that block direct connections (some LTE, office or school Wi‑Fi) won't connect with it on — turn it off there. The phone being controlled needs the latest app for this to fully apply.

The phone's owner stays in control

  • Remote access must be turned on by the phone's owner. While it's on, a notification is always shown and can stop it at once.
  • Adding a phone to your account needs the code shown on that phone.
  • A remote help link works once, for 10 minutes, connects only when the person allows it, and its access ends with the session. To keep the owner informed, turning the phone's screen off, reading its clipboard and root features aren't available on help-link connections.
  • No lock-screen bypass, no automatic clicks on system consent dialogs, and no stored PINs or passwords.

Protecting your account

  • Signing in with your email and password on a device for the first time asks for a six-digit code sent to your account email.
  • Changing your password signs out your other devices.
  • With Google sign-in, your Google account's 2-step verification applies. We email you when Google sign-in is added or removed.
  • NeulRemote never asks for your password or codes by email or phone.

Running the server

  • HTTPS for every connection, one-way hashed passwords, a firewalled server managed with keys only, and backups deleted automatically after 30 days.

Report a security issue

  • Found a problem? Please email support@neulremote.com.
