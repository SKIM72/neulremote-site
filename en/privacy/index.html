Privacy policy

Effective: 2026-11-22

This policy explains what NeulRemote (the Android app, the web app, the PC app, the NeulRemote server and this site) processes, and why.

What we process and why

  • Account: email address, name, password (the server keeps only a one-way hash), optional profile photo and role — to sign you in, show your devices and protect your account.
  • Google sign-in (only if you choose it): the email address and name Google has verified, and your Google account's ID — to sign you in and link your account. We don't receive your Google password, profile photo or anything else from your Google account.
  • Sign-in sessions: IP address, browser or app information (User-Agent) and time — to keep you signed in and to detect misuse.
  • Sign-in and connection records: the time of each sign-in (successful or failed, new-device codes, sign-outs and password changes) and of each internet (server-relayed) remote connection, the app and device name used, the phone connected to, how long it lasted and whether it was direct or relayed, and the first part of the IP address (e.g. 211.36.12.x — the last part is not kept) — to detect misuse and answer support requests. Deleted after 90 days. Connections over the same Wi-Fi or USB don't go through the server and are not recorded.
  • Registered devices: device name, maker and model, Android and app version, device type, remote-access status, battery, access level, last seen time and, on phones with Google Play services, a push address (Firebase Cloud Messaging token) — to list your phones, connect to them and wake a phone when you connect to it.
  • Remote connections: the connection details needed to link two devices. Screen, sound, input, voice calls and phone calls answered on another device travel encrypted between the devices and are not stored on the server. If a direct connection isn't possible, an encrypted relay (TURN) forwards the data without being able to read it.
  • Payments (if you buy Pro): for Google Play subscriptions, the purchase token, product, subscription state, expiry date, auto-renew status and order number — to check your Pro access and handle refunds. For App Store subscriptions, the original transaction ID, product, subscription state, expiry date, auto-renew status, storefront country and environment (production or sandbox) — to check your Pro access. For web payments (once available), the payment provider's reference to your payment method (billing key — stored encrypted), card issuer, partly masked card number, order number, payment time and amount — for renewals and refunds. We never receive card numbers or other payment details; Google, Apple and the payment provider handle them.
  • Remote help links: the link's status and a temporary device entry, removed when the session ends (10 minutes, single use).
  • Error reports: error message and code location, app version, a device summary (Android version, maker and model, or OS and browser) and your account if you're signed in — to fix problems. Emails, tokens and IP addresses are masked before saving, reports are deleted after 30 days, and you can turn them off in the app settings.
  • Email: sign-up verification and password reset emails.
  • Feedback: emails you send from your mail app and, if you choose, attached developer logs (with personal details masked) — to answer and fix issues.
  • Server logs: request addresses, times and IP addresses may appear in server logs for security and troubleshooting.

Kept only on your phone

  • Pairing keys, the phone app's activity log (who connected to this phone and when) and your settings stay on the device and are not sent to the server.

How long we keep it

  • Payment and subscription records (order numbers, subscription state, expiry dates, refunds) are kept as required by Korean e-commerce consumer protection law: payment and cancellation records for 5 years, complaint and dispute records for 3 years. If you delete your account, these records are unlinked from it, kept separately only for that period and then deleted.
  • Account information is kept until you delete your account. Deleting it removes the account and its registered devices, help links, sign-in and connection records, profile photo and push addresses right away; error reports lose their link to the account and are deleted after 30 days. A record that an account was deleted (an internal ID and the time, no email) is kept for security. Sessions end when you sign out or they expire. Sign-in and connection records are deleted after 90 days, error reports after 30 days.
  • The server is backed up daily to a separate disk for recovery. Backups are deleted automatically after 30 days, so deleted information may remain in backups for up to 30 days.

Sharing and other services

  • Payments: Google Play (payment and management of subscriptions bought on Play) and, once web payments start, a payment provider (Toss Payments — payment processing). Each handles only the information needed for payment under its own policy.
  • We don't use advertising or analytics tools, and we don't sell or share your information.
  • Services we rely on: Google Sign-In (only if you sign in with Google — to confirm it's you), Google Gmail (sending emails), Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (waking a phone when you connect; it receives the phone's push address and a “wake” signal, no screen content), DuckDNS (the server address) and GitHub (hosting this site and delivering app update files). This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which processes visitors' IP addresses.

Your choices and rights

  • You can sign out, unregister devices, turn off error reports and delete your account (Profile → Delete account, also in the web app — see how to delete your account). To view, correct or delete your information, or to stop its processing, you can also email us.
  • NeulRemote is not intended for children under 14 without a guardian's consent.

How we protect it

  • HTTPS for all connections, hashed passwords, encrypted remote sessions, access limited to the operator, a firewalled server and key-only administration.

Contact and changes

  • Privacy contact: NeulRemote operator — support@neulremote.com
  • If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
