Privacy policy

Effective: 2026-10-19

This policy explains what NeulRemote (the Android app, the web app, the PC app, the NeulRemote server and this site) processes, and why.

What we process and why

  • Account: email address, name, password (the server keeps only a one-way hash), optional profile photo and role — to sign you in, show your devices and protect your account.
  • Sign-in sessions: IP address, browser or app information (User-Agent) and time — to keep you signed in and to detect misuse.
  • Registered devices: device name, maker and model, Android and app version, device type, remote-access status, battery, access level and last seen time — to list your phones and connect to them.
  • Remote connections: the connection details needed to link two devices. Screen, sound, input and voice calls travel encrypted between the devices and are not stored on the server. If a direct connection isn't possible, an encrypted relay (TURN) forwards the data without being able to read it.
  • Remote help links: the link's status and a temporary device entry, removed when the session ends (10 minutes, single use).
  • Error reports: error message and code location, app version, a device summary (Android version, maker and model, or OS and browser) and your account if you're signed in — to fix problems. Emails, tokens and IP addresses are masked before saving, reports are deleted after 30 days, and you can turn them off in the app settings.
  • Email: sign-up verification and password reset emails.
  • Feedback: emails you send from your mail app and, if you choose, attached developer logs (with personal details masked) — to answer and fix issues.
  • Server logs: request addresses, times and IP addresses may appear in server logs for security and troubleshooting.

Kept only on your phone

  • Pairing keys, the access history (who connected and when) and your settings stay on the device and are not sent to the server.

How long we keep it

  • Account information is kept until you ask us to delete it. Sessions end when you sign out or they expire. Error reports are deleted after 30 days.
  • The server is backed up daily to a separate disk for recovery. Deleted information may remain in backups for a while.

Sharing and other services

  • We don't use advertising or analytics tools, and we don't sell or share your information.
  • Services we rely on: Google Gmail (sending emails), DuckDNS (the server address) and GitHub (hosting this site and delivering app update files). This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which processes visitors' IP addresses.

Your choices and rights

  • You can sign out, unregister devices and turn off error reports in the app. To view, correct or delete your information, or to stop its processing, email us.
  • NeulRemote is not intended for children under 14 without a guardian's consent.

How we protect it

  • HTTPS for all connections, hashed passwords, encrypted remote sessions, access limited to the operator, a firewalled server and key-only administration.

Contact and changes

  • Privacy contact: NeulRemote operator — neulremote.app@gmail.com
  • If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
