Six ways in

Near or far, there's a way to reach your phone.

Connect from anywhere

Register your phones to your account and connect over LTE or any other network. Phones connect directly when they can, with a relay as a fallback.

Instant on the same Wi‑Fi

Phones on the same Wi‑Fi appear on their own. Pair once by comparing six digits, then connect with one tap.

USB and wireless debugging

Mirror a phone with very low latency from a PC or Chrome/Edge — nothing to install on the phone. Record the screen and send files.

Control with the screen off

With Shizuku or root, keep the phone's own display off and work from the remote screen. Rooted phones can also be restarted and managed remotely.

Remote help link

Send a link in a chat. Once the other person allows it in the app, you can help with their phone this one time — no account needed on their side.

Voice call while you control

Talk with the person on the other side without leaving the remote screen. The microphone turns on only after they answer.

Every phone in one list

Phones registered to your account show up in one list, wherever they are. See which are online and how much battery they have, then connect.

  • Close by, device to device

    On the same Wi‑Fi, compare six digits once, then connect with one tap.

  • Far away, over the internet

    Connect over LTE or any network. If a direct link is blocked, an encrypted relay carries the session.

  • Wakes sleeping phones

    A phone on standby wakes up when you connect, even with its screen off (phones with Google Play services).

Help family far away with one link

Send a remote help link in a chat. Once the other person allows it in the app, you can see and help with their phone this one time.

  • No account on their side

    They only need the NeulRemote app: open the link, then tap Allow.

  • One use, ten minutes

    A link works once and expires after 10 minutes.

  • Access ends with the session

    A link never connects on its own, and access ends when the session does.

Talk while you control, screen off

Call the person holding the phone without leaving the remote screen. Sound output, the power button and file sending are in the same menu.

  • Mic only after they answer

    The microphone turns on only once the other person picks up.

  • The phone screen stays dark

    With Shizuku or root, keep the phone's display off and work from the remote screen.

  • Send files too

    Files you send from the remote screen are saved to Download/NeulRemote on the phone (up to 2 GB).

Three steps

  1. 1

    First, install it on the phone you want to reach.

    Remote access starts when the app opens, and its notification never hides.

  2. 2

    Then add that phone to your account.

    On the same Wi‑Fi it shows up without this. To reach it from anywhere, enter its six-digit code once.

  3. 3

    Now connect from any device.

    Tap Connect in the app on another phone, or in the web app on an iPhone or PC. A sleeping phone wakes up to answer.

How to use NeulRemote

You always decide

Remote access is always visible, and always yours to allow.

  • Never hidden

    Remote access is always shown in a notification and can be stopped from there.

  • Encrypted end to end

    Screen, sound and input travel encrypted between devices. The relay only forwards bytes and cannot read the session.

  • You stay in control

    New devices pair by comparing six digits. A help link never connects on its own — the person allows it, and access ends with the session.

  • No shortcuts around security

    No lock-screen bypass, no automatic clicks on system consent dialogs, and no stored PINs or passwords.

Common questions

Is it free?

Yes. NeulRemote is in preview and free to use.

What does the phone I want to control need?

Android 10 or later and the NeulRemote app. Screen sharing and accessibility permissions are enough to start; with Shizuku or root you get more, such as controlling with the screen off.

Can I control an iPhone?

No. iOS doesn't allow other apps to control it. iPhone and iPad can be the controlling side through the web app.

Can it unlock the phone for me?

No. NeulRemote never bypasses the lock screen and never stores PINs or passwords.

Is it on Google Play?

Not yet. Download the app from our page for now — it comes with step-by-step install help.

What data do you keep?

Only what's needed to run your account and connections. Screen contents are never stored. See the privacy policy for details.

Does it work if the phone is asleep?

Yes. A phone on standby wakes up when you connect, even with its screen off (phones with Google Play services). After a restart, unlock the phone once and standby comes back on.

Is it slow over the internet?

Devices connect directly whenever they can, which keeps the delay short. Only on networks that block a direct link does the session go through the encrypted relay.

What more can a rooted phone do?

Besides working with the screen off, you can restart it, turn Wi‑Fi and mobile data on or off, and use a remote shell and logcat. Each must be allowed separately in the phone's settings and is recorded in its activity log.
