Terms of Service

Effective: 2026-11-14

These terms set the conditions for using NeulRemote (the Android app, web app, PC app and NeulRemote server — the "service") and the rights and duties of the operator and users.

The service

  • The service lets you see and control an Android phone's screen from another phone, a PC or a browser — over the same Wi‑Fi or USB, or over the internet through your account.
  • We work to keep the service reliable, but connections may fail or some features may be limited depending on devices, networks, operating systems and outside services such as Google.

Your account

  • Give accurate information when you sign up and keep your password and signed-in devices safe. If you think your account has been misused, change your password right away and tell us.
  • Children under 14 may not sign up without a parent's or guardian's consent.

Only connect to devices you may use

  • Only connect to your own devices or devices whose owner has allowed it. Don't use the service to secretly access someone else's device or to watch or record other people's conversations, calls or personal information.
  • Don't break the law, damage the service or its servers, disrupt other users or pretend to be someone else.
  • If you do, we may suspend your use or delete your account after telling you (in urgent cases we stop it first and tell you right away).

Plans

  • Free: basic control (screen, touch, keyboard, sound, calls, messages), unlimited same‑Wi‑Fi and USB connections, 1 phone over the internet (through your account), and sending files up to 100 MB each.
  • Pro: everything in Free plus up to 10 phones over the internet, game mode and key mapping, choosing sound output, screen recording and files up to 2 GB each. Pro belongs to your account, so it works the same in the Android app, the web app and PC browsers. The features in each plan are listed on the pricing section of this site, and we may add features to either plan.
  • Prices are shown on this site and in the purchase screen, including applicable taxes. Accounts created before the official launch get Pro free for 3 months after launch.

Subscriptions and renewal

  • Pro is a subscription that renews automatically every month or year. Unless you cancel, you are charged the same price when each period ends.
  • Your first subscription on Google Play starts with a 7-day free trial. Cancel during the trial and you won't be charged; otherwise the first charge happens when the trial ends. Web subscriptions have no free trial; instead you can refund a web charge yourself within 7 days (see Refunds).
  • Web payments (subscribing in the web app): the card you register through Toss Payments is charged when you subscribe and then automatically on the same day each month or year (the last day of the month when that day doesn't exist). If a renewal fails, we try again after 1 and 3 days and you keep Pro meanwhile (until 3 days after the period ends); if it still fails, the subscription ends and your account returns to the free plan. For yearly subscriptions we email you 7 days before renewal.
  • Cancel: subscriptions bought on Google Play are canceled in the Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions; subscriptions bought on the web are canceled in your profile, at any time. You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • We announce price changes at least 30 days before they apply and ask for your consent to increases. If you don't agree, the subscription ends when the current period ends.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Please cancel it before deleting your account.

Refunds

  • Within 7 days of a charge (including the first charge after a free trial) we refund it in full, no questions asked.
  • After 7 days: monthly subscriptions stop at the next renewal when you cancel, and the current month is not refunded. Yearly subscriptions are refunded for the remaining full months (yearly price ÷ 12 × remaining months, not counting the current month).
  • How to ask: for Google Play purchases you can request a refund in Google Play yourself within 48 hours; after that, email support@neulremote.com and we refund under the rules above. Web purchases can be refunded right away within 7 days of the charge with [Refund and cancel] in Profile → Plan; after that, email the same address. Refunds go back to the original payment method and may take 3–7 business days with the payment provider.
  • If you couldn't use the service because of our fault, or we end a paid service, we refund the unused period.
  • A refund ends Pro from that subscription right away.

Changes and interruptions

  • We announce feature changes and maintenance in advance on the site and in the app. Urgent security issues may be fixed first and announced afterwards.
  • If we end a paid service, we tell you at least 30 days in advance and refund the remaining period.

Liability

  • We compensate damage we cause intentionally or negligently, as the law requires.
  • We are not responsible for damage caused by things we can't prevent, such as problems with your devices or network or natural disasters, except where the law says otherwise.
  • What happens during remote control (opening apps, sending messages, changing settings and so on) is done by the person controlling the phone. Check with the phone's owner before anything important.

Deleting your account

  • You can delete your account at any time in your profile (how to delete your account). What is deleted and what is kept follows the privacy policy.

Changes to these terms and disputes

  • We post changes on this site at least 7 days before they take effect (30 days if they are unfavorable to you). If you don't agree, you can stop using the service and delete your account.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Korea, and disputes go to the court with jurisdiction under the Korean Civil Procedure Act. Please email support@neulremote.com first and we will respond in good faith.
