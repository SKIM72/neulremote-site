Control from an Android phone

Install NeulRemote on the controlling phone too (same as step 1 of the preparation). One app does both: controlling and being controlled.

First, get the Android phone you'll control ready → Prepare the phone

On the same Wi‑Fi

  1. On the controlling phone, phones on the same Wi‑Fi appear in the Connect tab. Tap Connect. If yours is missing, pull down to search again.
  2. On the first connection, both phones show the same 6-digit number. Tap Allow on the controlled phone only if the numbers match.
  3. At the basic level (T0), you approve screen sharing on every connection. Tap Start now. On Android 14 or later, choose the entire screen.
  4. Once connected, use the bottom bar for Back, Home, Recents, volume, sound, and power. The magnifier turns on zoom, and the red button on the right disconnects.

From anywhere over the internet

  1. Add both phones to the same account (preparation step 4).
  2. In the Devices tab, pick the phone and tap Connect via internet. Phones that are off show a dimmed button.
  3. The phones connect directly when they can, and through a relay server when they can't. The dot under the remote screen shows the connection quality.

Remote help link (no account needed, one time)

  1. In the Devices tab, tap Send a remote help link and send the link in a chat or text.
  2. When the other person opens it, the NeulRemote app (or the install page) shows who sent it, and they tap Allow. It connects right away.
  3. A link works once within 10 minutes, and the access ends with the session.

Using the remote screen

  1. Toolbar: Back, Home, Recents, volume, sound, power, keyboard, zoom and disconnect (red).
  2. Hide the toolbar for a bigger screen — the small button left behind can be dragged anywhere.
  3. Start a voice call while you control. The microphone turns on only after the other phone answers.
